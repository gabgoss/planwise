## 6. Discovery-Phase Consolidation — Verify Citations and SDK Premises Against Live Source

A Discovery or consolidation agent treats every `file:line` citation and third-party SDK premise in its task brief as a starting hypothesis, verifies it against live source, and flags a false premise as a prominent correction.
